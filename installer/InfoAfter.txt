Sentinel is installed and ready.

On first run, the Dashboard will offer to install the ClamAV engine. That
downloads the official package and verifies its SHA-256 digest. You will then
be prompted to fetch virus signatures (a large first download).

After that, try a Quick scan from the Scan page - it covers Downloads, Desktop
and Documents, and splits large folders across several engine processes for
speed. Everything Sentinel has ever detected is listed on the Threats page.

Sentinel is on-demand only. Keep Microsoft Defender enabled.
